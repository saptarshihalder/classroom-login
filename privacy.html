Privacy policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

What Course Boards does

Course Boards lets a person enrolled in a Google Classroom course choose course announcements, classwork materials and files to publish on a board that anyone can read.

Google data we access

After you sign in and give permission, the service may access:

  • your basic Google profile and email address;
  • courses, rosters, announcements, topics and classwork materials available to your account; and
  • Google Drive files connected to material you choose to publish.

We use this access only to show you eligible courses, prepare your selections and publish the board you request. Course Boards does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

What becomes public

Only content you select and then publish is placed on a public board. Published course details, posts and file copies can be viewed by anyone with the board link. Review your selections before publishing and do not publish private, confidential or copyrighted material without permission.

Storage and retention

Published board data and file copies are stored using Cloudflare services so the public board can operate. Authentication information is used to complete the requested sync. We retain published content while its board remains available or as needed to operate and protect the service.

Removal and questions

To request removal of a board or its stored data, or to ask a privacy question, email saptarshidoingthesis@gmail.com. Include the board link and enough information to identify your request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be published on this page.
